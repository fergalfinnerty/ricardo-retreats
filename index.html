Ask Ricard about joining How the days feel

The experience

Movement, stillness, and time together

Days are shaped gently: active morning practice, quieter evenings, and space to rest or explore. Classes are beginner-friendly and optional — you are welcome to take part in as much or as little as feels right.

Morning energy

Active morning yoga to wake the body. Sessions are welcoming for beginners and paced with care.

Evening ease

Gentler restorative or yin practice, with breathwork, meditation and relaxing sound when the day softens.

Shared meals

Three chef-prepared meals each day. Vegetarian and vegan needs, and allergies, can be discussed with Ricard.

Free time

Room to rest, read, or simply sit with the sea. Optional island walks and exploration may be possible — not guaranteed included excursions.

Community

A sincere, small-group atmosphere. Come alone or with someone — many people arrive by themselves and find easy company.

Your pace

Participation is optional. Mats are available; bringing your own can be useful if you prefer it.

An indicative rhythm from earlier planning: morning and evening sessions of roughly 75–90 minutes, with workshop themes such as backbends/heart-openers and inversions possible. Treat this as a sense of the flow, not a confirmed timetable.

Meet your host

Ricard Chillon

Ricard is a personal trainer and yoga teacher who builds community with warmth and care. Friends describe him as sincere, empathetic, positive, thoughtful and proactive — someone who notices people and makes space for them.

This retreat is his invitation to step out of everyday noise for five nights: move, rest, eat well, and share quiet company by the sea.

Follow updates and ask questions on Instagram @ricardchillonpt.

The villa

Northern Lanzarote, close to the sea

A coastal villa near the natural pools, with a yoga studio that looks out to the sea and communal spaces for lingering after practice. Five rooms with two beds each, as described in the retreat promotion. Exact address and room assignments are confirmed with Ricard.

Food & the kitchen

Three chef-prepared meals a day are included. This is not an exclusively vegetarian retreat; vegetarian and vegan preferences, and allergies, can be discussed when you enquire.

The kitchen is led by the chef at @maurosiepechef together with his wife. Please ask Ricard for the latest details when you get in touch.

FAQs

I’m a beginner — is this for me?

Yes. Practices are beginner-friendly, and classes are optional. You can take part at your own pace.

Can I come alone?

Absolutely. Many people do. The atmosphere is welcoming and community-minded.

What about dietary needs?

Vegetarian and vegan preferences, and allergies, can be discussed with Ricard when you enquire. The retreat is not exclusively vegetarian.

Do I need to bring a yoga mat?

Mats are available. Bringing your own can be useful if you have a favourite.

How do I enquire or book?
